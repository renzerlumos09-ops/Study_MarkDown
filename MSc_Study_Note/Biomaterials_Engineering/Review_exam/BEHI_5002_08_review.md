## 选择题

**1. What are the two fundamental properties of stem cells?** **干细胞的两个基本特性是什么？**

- A. Proliferation and Apoptosis. (增殖与凋亡。)
- B. Self-renewal and Differentiation. (自更新与分化。)
- C. Migration and Adhesion. (迁移与粘附。)
- D. Mutation and Selection. (突变与选择。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Self-renewal allows stem cells to make more of themselves, and differentiation allows them to become specialized cell types. (中) 自更新使干细胞能够产生更多自身，而分化使它们能够变成专门的细胞类型。

**2. Which type of stem cell can differentiate into all cell types, including embryonic and extra-embryonic tissues?** **哪种类型的干细胞可以分化为所有细胞类型，包括胚胎和胚外组织？**

- A. Pluripotent (多能)
- B. Multipotent (专能)
- C. Totipotent (全能)
- D. Unipotent (单能)
- **答案：C**
- **解析：** (En) Totipotent cells (like a zygote) have the highest potential. (中) 全能细胞（如受精卵）具有最高的潜能。**[补充考点]**：多能(Pluripotent)细胞只能分化为三个胚层的所有细胞，不包括胎盘。

**3. Hematopoietic stem cells found in bone marrow are examples of:** **骨髓中的造血干细胞是以下哪种例子：**

- A. Totipotent cells (全能细胞)
- B. Pluripotent cells (多能细胞)
- C. Multipotent cells (专能/多能性细胞)
- D. Adult stem cells (成年干细胞)
- **答案：C (及D)**
- **解析：** (En) They are multipotent because they can develop into more than one cell type, but are limited to a specific lineage (blood cells). (中) 它们是专能的，因为它们可以发育出多种细胞，但仅限于特定谱系（血细胞）。

**4. What is the primary source of Embryonic Stem Cells (ESCs)?** **胚胎干细胞 (ESCs) 的主要来源是什么？**

- C. Umbilical cord blood. (脐带血。)
- D. Skin biopsy. (皮肤活检。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) ESCs are derived from embryos that are 3-5 days old. (中) ESCs来源于3-5天大的胚胎。

## 名词解释

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Self-renewal (自更新)**: The unique ability of stem cells to divide and produce more cells of the same type, thereby maintaining the pool of undifferentiated cells in the body or in culture. （干细胞分裂并产生更多相同类型细胞的独特能力，从而维持体内或培养物中未分化细胞的数量。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Symmetric Division (对称分裂)* —— 产生两个相同的干细胞。
2. **Differentiation (分化)**: The biological process by which a unspecialized stem cell becomes a more specialized cell type (e.g., neuron, muscle cell) with specific functions. （未专门化的干细胞变成具有特定功能的更专门化细胞类型（如神经元、肌肉细胞）的生物过程。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Lineage (谱系)* —— 细胞分化所遵循的特定路径（如造血谱系）。
3. **Pluripotency (多能性)**: The capacity of a stem cell to differentiate into any cell type from the three germ layers (endoderm, mesoderm, and ectoderm), but not extra-embryonic tissues like the placenta. （干细胞分化为三胚层（内、中、外胚层）中任何细胞类型的能力，但不包括胎盘等胚外组织。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Totipotency (全能性)* —— 潜能更高，可形成完整生物体。
4. **Stem Cell Niche (干细胞龛)**: The specialized microenvironment that surrounds stem cells, providing physical and chemical signals that regulate their participation in tissue generation, maintenance, and repair. （包裹干细胞的特定微环境，提供调节其参与组织生成、维持和修复的物理和化学信号。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Quiescence (静止态)* —— 干细胞在“龛”中保持休眠、不分裂的状态。
5. **Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells (iPSCs) (诱导多能干细胞)**: Adult somatic cells (such as skin cells) that have been genetically reprogrammed to an embryonic-like state, gaining the ability to differentiate into all cell types. （通过基因重编程被还原到类胚胎状态的成年体细胞（如皮肤细胞），从而获得分化为所有细胞类型的能力。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Reprogramming (重编程)* —— 将分化细胞逆转回多能状态的过程。
6. **Surface Topography (表面形貌)**: The physical structure and patterns (e.g., grooves, pits, or pillars) on a biomaterial surface that influence cell shape and guide stem cell fate through mechanical sensing. （生物材料表面的物理结构和图案（如沟槽、凹坑或柱子），通过机械感知影响细胞形状并引导干细胞命运。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Contact Guidance (接触引导)* —— 细胞沿表面纹理定向生长的现象。
7. **Substrate Stiffness (基质刚性)**: A mechanical property of the material (often measured by Young's modulus) that cells "feel" to direct their differentiation; for example, soft substrates promote neurogenesis. （材料的一种机械特性（通常由杨氏模量衡量），细胞通过“感知”该特性来引导其分化；例如，软基质促进神经发生。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Mechanotransduction (机械信号转导)* —— 细胞将物理刚性转化为生化信号的过程。
8. **Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs) (间充质干细胞)**: Multipotent adult stem cells found in tissues like bone marrow that can differentiate into mesodermal lineages, including bone, cartilage, and fat. （存在于骨髓等组织中的专能成年干细胞，能够分化为中胚层谱系，包括骨、软骨和脂肪。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Osteogenesis (成骨分化)* —— MSCs 在硬基质上分化为骨细胞。

------

### 关联重要名词补充 (Additional Important Terms)

- **Embryonic Stem Cells (ESCs) (胚胎干细胞)**：源自囊胚内细胞群 (Inner Cell Mass)，具有多能性，但存在伦理 (Ethics)争议。
- **Anisotropy (各向异性)**：材料在不同方向上具有不同性质，如对齐的纤维 (Aligned fibers)，可引导神经轴突定向生长。
- **Bioactive Molecules (生物活性分子)**：如生长因子，作为化学信号 (Chemical Cues) 添加到支架中以诱导分化。

## True&False

### 第三部分：判断题 (True & False Questions - 20题)

1. **Stem cells are the only cells in the body that can make different cell types.** **干细胞是体内唯一能产生不同细胞类型的细胞。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Specialized cells like muscle or nerve cells cannot change into other types naturally. (中) 肌肉或神经细胞等专门化细胞无法自然转变为其他类型。
2. **Self-renewal and differentiation are the two hallmark properties of stem cells.** **自更新和分化是干细胞的两个标志性特性。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) These define a stem cell's ability to maintain its population and functionalize tissues. (中) 这定义了干细胞维持其群体规模和使组织功能化的能力。
3. **Pluripotent stem cells have the potential to form an entire organism, including the placenta.** **多能干细胞具有形成整个生物体（包括胎盘）的潜能。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Only totipotent cells can form the placenta; pluripotent cells form all embryo cell types but not the placenta. (中) 只有全能细胞能形成胎盘；多能细胞形成胚胎的所有细胞类型，但不包括胎盘。
4. **Adult stem cells are found in almost all tissues of the body.** **成年干细胞几乎存在于身体的所有组织中。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) They act as a repair system for the tissue in which they reside. (中) 它们作为其所在组织的修复系统发挥作用。
5. **A "Soft" substrate (~0.5 kPa) is ideal for promoting bone cell (osteogenic) differentiation.** **“软”基质（~0.5 kPa）是促进骨细胞（成骨）分化的理想选择。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Soft substrates promote neurogenesis (nerve); hard substrates promote osteogenesis (bone). (中) 软基质促进神经发生；硬基质促进成骨。
6. **The stem cell "Niche" provides only chemical signals to the cells.** **干细胞“龛”仅向细胞提供化学信号。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) The niche provides both chemical (growth factors) and physical (stiffness, topography) signals. (中) “龛”同时提供化学（生长因子）和物理（刚性、形貌）信号。
7. **Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells (iPSCs) avoid many ethical issues associated with Embryonic Stem Cells.** **诱导多能干细胞 (iPSCs) 避免了许多与胚胎干细胞相关的伦理问题。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) iPSCs are derived from adult skin or blood cells, not embryos. (中) iPSCs源自成年皮肤或血液细胞，而非胚胎。
8. **Surface topography like nano-grooves can guide the direction of stem cell growth.** **纳米沟槽等表面形貌可以引导干细胞生长的方向。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) This is known as contact guidance. (中) 这被称为接触引导。

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Quiescence is a dormant, non-dividing state to protect the stem cell pool. (中) 静止态是保护干细胞库的一种休眠、不分裂状态。

## 简答

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions)

**1. Compare the differentiation potential of Totipotent, Pluripotent, and Multipotent stem cells.** **比较全能、多能和专能干细胞的分化潜能。**

- **答案：**
  - **Totipotent (全能):** Can differentiate into all cell types including embryonic and extra-embryonic (placenta) tissues. (能分化为所有细胞类型，包括胚胎和胚外（胎盘）组织。)
  - **Pluripotent (多能):** Can differentiate into all cells of the three germ layers (endoderm, mesoderm, ectoderm), but not the placenta. (能分化为三个胚层的所有细胞，但不能分化为胎盘。)
  - **Multipotent (专能):** Can differentiate into multiple cell types within a specific lineage (e.g., blood or bone). (能在特定谱系内分化为多种细胞，如血液或骨骼。)

**2. How does substrate stiffness influence the differentiation of Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs)?** **基质刚性如何影响间充质干细胞 (MSCs) 的分化？**

- **答案：** Cells sense the mechanical rigidity of the substrate. Soft substrates (~0.1-1 kPa) mimic brain tissue and promote neurogenesis (nerve cells). Intermediate stiffness (~10 kPa) mimics muscle and promotes myogenesis. Hard/Stiff substrates (~25-40 kPa) mimic bone and promote osteogenesis. (细胞感知基质的机械刚性。软基质促进神经发生；中等刚性促进生肌；硬基质促进成骨。)

**3. What are Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells (iPSCs) and what is their advantage over Embryonic Stem Cells (ESCs)?** **什么是诱导多能干细胞 (iPSCs)？它们相比胚胎干细胞 (ESCs) 的优势是什么？**

- **答案：** iPSCs are adult somatic cells that have been genetically reprogrammed to a pluripotent state. (iPSCs是经过基因重编程达到多能状态的成年体细胞。)
  - **Advantages (优势):** They avoid ethical controversies (no embryos destroyed) and reduce immune rejection because they can be made from the patient's own cells. (它们避免了伦理争议（不破坏胚胎），并减少了免疫排斥，因为可以用患者自身的细胞制备。)

**4. Describe the role of "Surface Topography" in nerve tissue engineering.** **描述“表面形貌”在神经组织工程中的作用。**

- **答案：** Physical features like micro-grooves or aligned fibers provide contact guidance for cells. This guides the direction of axonal growth and helps reestablish nerve connections. For example, multi-microtubule structures favor neuronal differentiation over flat films. (微沟槽或排列的纤维等物理特征为细胞提供接触引导。这引导了轴突生长的方向，有助于重建神经连接。例如，多微管结构比扁平薄膜更有利于神经元分化。)

**5. Why is the "Stem Cell Niche" important for maintaining stem cell function in the body?** **为什么“干细胞龛”对于维持体内干细胞功能很重要？**

- **答案：** The niche is a specialized microenvironment that provides biochemical cues and physical support. It regulates whether stem cells remain in quiescence (dormancy) to preserve the pool, or undergo self-renewal and differentiation for tissue repair. (龛是一个提供生化信号和物理支撑的特定微环境。它调节干细胞是保持静止（休眠）以保存细胞库，还是进行自更新和分化以修复组织。)
