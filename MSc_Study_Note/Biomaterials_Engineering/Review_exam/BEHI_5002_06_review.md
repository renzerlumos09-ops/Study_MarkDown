**1. What is the main goal of a "Drug Delivery System (DDS)"?** **药物递送系统 (DDS) 的主要目标是什么？**

**2. Which of the following is a key impediment to successful drug delivery?** **以下哪项是成功药物递送的主要障碍？**

## 名词解释

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Therapeutic Window (治疗窗口)**: The range of drug concentrations in the blood between the minimum effective concentration (MEC) and the minimum toxic concentration (MTC). （血液中药物浓度的范围，介于最低有效浓度 (MEC) 和最低中毒浓度 (MTC) 之间。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Bioavailability (生物利用度)* —— 实际到达体循环的药物比例。
2. **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) (血脑屏障)**: A highly selective semipermeable border that prevents solutes in the circulating blood from non-selectively crossing into the extracellular fluid of the central nervous system. （一种具有高度选择性的半透性边界，防止循环血液中的溶质非选择性地进入中枢神经系统的细胞外液。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Tight Junctions (紧密连接)* —— 构成 BBB 物理屏障的细胞结构。
3. **EPR Effect (增强的通透性和滞留效应)**: The property by which molecules of certain sizes (typically nanoparticles) tend to accumulate in tumor tissue much more than in normal tissues due to leaky vasculature. （由于血管渗漏，特定大小的分子（通常是纳米颗粒）倾向于在肿瘤组织中积聚，且远多于正常组织的特性。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Passive Targeting (被动靶向)* —— 利用 EPR 效应实现的自然积聚。
4. **PEGylation (聚乙二醇化)**: The covalent attachment of Polyethylene Glycol (PEG) polymer chains to another molecule (like a nanoparticle), which increases circulation time by evading the immune system. （将聚乙二醇 (PEG) 聚合物链共价结合到另一个分子（如纳米颗粒）上，通过逃避免疫系统来增加循环时间。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Stealth Liposome (隐身脂质体)* —— 经过 PEG 修饰的典型长循环载体。
5. **Amphiphilic (两亲性)**: A chemical compound possessing both hydrophilic (water-loving) and lipophilic (fat-loving/hydrophobic) properties, essential for forming micelles and liposomes. （一种同时具有亲水性和亲脂性（疏水性）特性的化合物，是形成胶束和脂质体的基础。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC, 临界胶束浓度)* —— 开始形成胶束的最低浓度。
6. **Diffusion-Controlled Release (扩散控制释放)**: A mechanism where the drug release rate is determined by the movement of drug molecules through a polymer matrix or membrane, following Fick's Law. （一种释放机制，其中药物释放速率由药物分子通过聚合物基质或膜的运动决定，遵循菲克定律。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Reservoir System (储库型系统)* —— 典型的扩散控制释放模型。
7. **Stimuli-Responsive (刺激响应性)**: The ability of a drug delivery system to change its properties (e.g., shape, solubility) and release drugs in response to specific environmental triggers like pH, temperature, or enzymes. （药物递送系统能够响应特定的环境诱因（如 pH、温度或酶）而改变其特性（如形状、溶解度）并释放药物的能力。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Smart Polymers (智能聚合物)* —— 实现刺激响应的材料基础。
8. **Encapsulation Efficiency (EE) (封装效率)**: The percentage of the total amount of drug added that is successfully trapped inside the delivery vehicle (nanoparticles/liposomes). （加入的药物总量中成功捕获在递送载体（纳米颗粒/脂质体）内部的百分比。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Drug Loading (载药量)* —— 单位重量载体所含的药物质量。

### 第三部分：判断题 (True & False Questions - 20题)

1. **Zero-order release provides a drug concentration that decreases exponentially over time.****零级释放提供的药物浓度随时间呈指数级下降。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Zero-order release provides a **constant** release rate; first-order release decreases over time. (中) 零级释放提供的是**恒定**的释放速率；一级释放才随时间下降。

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions)

**1. Explain the difference between "Passive Targeting" and "Active Targeting" in cancer therapy.****解释癌症治疗中“被动靶向”和“主动靶向”的区别。**

- **答案：** Passive Targeting relies on the EPR effect, where nanoparticles naturally accumulate in tumor tissue through leaky blood vessels. Active Targeting involves attaching specific ligands (e.g., antibodies) to the carrier surface to recognize and bind to specific receptors on target cells. (被动靶向依赖于 EPR 效应，即纳米颗粒通过渗漏的血管自然积聚在肿瘤组织中。主动靶向涉及在载体表面附着特定配体（如抗体），以识别并结合靶细胞上的特定受体。)

**2. Describe the two main mechanisms of polymer-controlled drug release.****描述聚合物控制药物释放的两种主要机制。**

- **答案：**
  1. Diffusion-controlled: The drug moves through the polymer matrix or membrane from high to low concentration (Fick’s Law). (扩散控制：药物从高浓度向低浓度通过聚合物基质或膜移动。)
  2. Erosion/Degradation-controlled: The drug is released as the polymer carrier breaks down or dissolves physically or chemically. (侵蚀/降解控制：随着聚合物载体物理或化学分解或溶解，药物被释放。)

**3. What is "PEGylation" and why is it used for nanoparticles in drug delivery?****什么是“聚乙二醇化”？为什么它被用于药物递送中的纳米颗粒？**

- **答案：** PEGylation is the covalent attachment of PEG chains to the nanoparticle surface. It is used to create a "stealth" effect, which prevents opsonization (binding of immune proteins) and reduces clearance by the RES system (liver/spleen), thereby increasing circulation time in the blood. (聚乙二醇化是将 PEG 链共价结合到纳米颗粒表面。它用于产生“隐身”效应，防止调理作用（免疫蛋白结合）并减少 RES 系统（肝/脾）的清除，从而增加血液循环时间。)

**4. Compare "Zero-order" and "First-order" drug release kinetics.****比较“零级”和“一级”药物释放动力学。**

- **答案：** Zero-order release has a constant release rate that is independent of the remaining drug concentration (ideal for maintaining steady blood levels). First-order release has a rate that decreases over time as it is proportional to the concentration of drug remaining in the system. (零级释放具有恒定的释放速率，与剩余药物浓度无关（是维持稳定血药浓度的理想选择）。一级释放的速率随时间降低，因为它与系统中剩余的药物浓度成正比。)

**5. Name three biological barriers that a drug delivery system must overcome.****列举药物递送系统必须克服的三个生物屏障。**

- **答案：**
  1. Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB): Prevents entry into the central nervous system. (血脑屏障：防止进入中枢神经系统。)
  2. Gastrointestinal (GI) Barrier: Harsh low pH and enzymes that can degrade drugs. (胃肠道屏障：可能降解药物的严酷低 pH 值和酶。)
  3. Immune System/RES: The liver and spleen clear foreign particles from circulation. (免疫系统/RES：肝脏和脾脏清除循环中的外来颗粒。)
