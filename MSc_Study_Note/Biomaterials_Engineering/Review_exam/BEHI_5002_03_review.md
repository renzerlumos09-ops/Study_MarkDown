### 第一部分：多选题 (Multiple Choice Questions - 40题)

**1. What is the very first event that occurs immediately after a biomaterial is implanted into the vascularized tissue?** **生物材料植入有血管的组织后，最先发生的事件是什么？**

- A. Cell migration (细胞迁移)
- B. Protein adsorption (蛋白质吸附)
- C. Fibrotic encapsulation (纤维包缩)
- D. Tumor formation (肿瘤形成)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Within nanoseconds, proteins from blood/fluids adsorb onto the surface. (中) 在纳秒内，来自血液/体液的蛋白质就会吸附在材料表面。这是后续所有生物反应的基础。

**2. The "Vroman Effect" refers to:** **“Vroman 效应”是指：**

- A. The way cells eat the material. (细胞吞噬材料的方式。)
- B. The hierarchical displacement of adsorbed proteins over time based on their affinity and concentration. (吸附蛋白质随时间根据其亲和力和浓度发生的层级替换。)
- C. The process of metal rusting in the body. (金属在体内生锈的过程。)
- D. The growth of bacteria on the surface. (细菌在表面的生长。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Early adsorbed proteins (like albumin) are replaced by those with higher affinity (like fibrinogen or kininogen). (中) 早期吸附的蛋白质（如白蛋白）会被亲和力更高的蛋白质（如纤维蛋白原或激肽原）取代。

**3. Which cell type is the first to arrive at the injury site during the acute inflammation phase?** **在急性炎症阶段，哪种细胞最先到达损伤部位？**

- A. Macrophages (巨噬细胞)
- B. Fibroblasts (成纤维细胞)
- C. Neutrophils (中性粒细胞)
- D. Lymphocytes (淋巴细胞)
- **答案：C**
- **解析：** (En) Neutrophils are the first responders, arriving within hours to clear debris and bacteria. (中) 中性粒细胞是第一响应者，在数小时内到达以清除碎片和细菌。

**4. What is the main function of "Foreign Body Giant Cells" (FBGCs)?** **“异物巨细胞” (FBGCs) 的主要功能是什么？**

- A. To transport oxygen. (运输氧气。)
- B. To attempt to phagocytose large biomaterials by fusing multiple macrophages together. (通过融合多个巨噬细胞来尝试吞噬大型生物材料。)
- C. To create new bone. (制造新骨。)
- D. To kill bacteria. (杀死细菌。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) FBGCs form when a material is too large for a single macrophage to ingest. (中) 当材料太大，单个巨噬细胞无法摄取时，就会形成 FBGC。

**5. "Fibrotic Encapsulation" is considered:** **“纤维包缩”被认为是：**

- A. A successful integration of the material. (材料的成功整合。)
- B. The final stage of the foreign body response to a non-degradable material. (对不可降解材料异物反应的最终阶段。)
- C. A sign of cancer. (癌症的征兆。)
- D. A way to deliver drugs. (一种递送药物的方式。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) The body "walls off" the material with a dense layer of collagen and fibroblasts. (中) 身体用致密的胶原蛋白和成纤维细胞层将材料“隔离”开。

**6. Which pathway of the Complement System is activated directly by the biomaterial surface?** **补体系统的哪条途径可由生物材料表面直接激活？**

- A. Classical pathway (经典途径)
- B. Alternative pathway (旁路/替代途径)
- C. Lectin pathway (凝集素途径)
- D. None of the above (以上均不是)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) The alternative pathway is often triggered by hydroxyl or amino groups on the material surface. (中) 旁路途径通常由材料表面的羟基或氨基触发。

**7. "Hemostasis" (止血) involves which of the following stages?** **“止血”涉及以下哪些阶段？**

- A. Vascular constriction, platelet plug formation, and blood coagulation. (血管收缩、血小板栓形成和血液凝固。)
- B. Cell death only. (仅细胞死亡。)
- C. Muscle growth. (肌肉生长。)
- D. Nerve regeneration. (神经再生。)
- **答案：A**
- **解析：** (En) This is the body's immediate response to prevent blood loss. (中) 这是身体防止失血的立即反应。

**8. The "Intrinsic Pathway" of blood coagulation is triggered by:** **血液凝固的“内源性途径”是由什么触发的？**

- A. Tissue factor (TF) from damaged tissue. (来自受损组织的组织因子 TF。)

**9. What is the role of "Thrombin" (凝血酶) in the coagulation cascade?** **凝血酶在凝血级联反应中的作用是什么？**

- A. It dissolves the clot. (它溶解血块。)
- B. It converts soluble Fibrinogen into insoluble Fibrin. (它将可溶性纤维蛋白原转化为不可溶性纤维蛋白。)
- C. It produces white blood cells. (它产生白细胞。)
- D. It carries cholesterol. (它携带胆固醇。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Fibrin forms the mesh-like structure of the blood clot. (中) 纤维蛋白构成了血块的网状结构。

**10. "Acute Toxicity" refers to harmful effects occurring within:** **“急性毒性”是指发生在多久时间内的有害效应？**

- A. 10 years. (10年。)

- **答案：B**

**11. "Mutagenicity" (诱变性) tests look for:** **“诱变性”测试寻找的是：**

- A. Changes in blood pressure. (血压的变化。)

**12. What is "Aseptic Inflammation"?** **什么是“无菌性炎症”？**

- A. Inflammation caused by a virus. (由病毒引起的炎症。)
- B. Inflammation caused by the material itself or its wear debris without infection. (在没有感染的情况下，由材料本身或其磨损碎片引起的炎症。)

**13. A "Hapten" (半抗原) is:** **“半抗原”是：**

- A. A complete virus. (一个完整的病毒。)

**14. What is the difference between "Innate" and "Adaptive" immunity?** **“先天性免疫”和“适应性免疫”有什么区别？**

**15. "Opsonization" (调理作用) is the process of:** **“调理作用”的过程是：**

**22. "Oppenheimer Effect" (Solid-state carcinogenesis) suggests that:** **“Oppenheimer 效应”（固态致癌）认为：**

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Vroman Effect (Vroman 效应)**: The hierarchical displacement of adsorbed proteins on a surface over time, where proteins with higher concentrations but lower affinity (like albumin) are eventually replaced by proteins with higher affinity (like fibrinogen or high-molecular-weight kininogen). （表面吸附蛋白质的层级替换现象，即浓度高但亲和力低的蛋白质（如白蛋白）最终会被亲和力更高的蛋白质（如纤维蛋白原或高分子量激肽原）所取代。）
   - **关联重点**：*Protein Adsorption (蛋白吸附)* —— 决定了后续细胞如何识别表面。
2. **Hemostasis (止血)**: The body's immediate physiological response to stop bleeding at the site of injury, involving vascular constriction, platelet plug formation, and blood coagulation (clotting). （身体在损伤部位停止流血的即时生理反应，包括血管收缩、血小板血栓形成和血液凝固。）
   - **关联重点**：*Thrombus (血栓)* —— 血液接触材料时的一种非预期止血结果。
3. **Foreign Body Reaction (FBR, 异物反应)**: A special form of chronic inflammation characterized by the presence of macrophages, Foreign Body Giant Cells (FBGCs), and the formation of fibrotic encapsulation around a non-degradable material. （一种特殊形式的慢性炎症，其特征是存在巨噬细胞、异物巨细胞 (FBGCs)，并在不可降解材料周围形成纤维包囊。）
   - **关联重点**：*Macrophage Fusion (巨噬细胞融合)* —— 形成 FBGC 的关键步骤。
4. **Complement System (补体系统)**: A part of the innate immune system consisting of a cascade of plasma proteins that can be activated by biomaterials (primarily via the alternative pathway) to promote inflammation and clear foreign substances. （先天免疫系统的一部分，由一系列等离子体蛋白质级联组成，可被生物材料（主要通过旁路途径）激活，以促进炎症并清除外来物质。）
   - **关联重点**：*Opsonization (调理作用)* —— 补体蛋白标记外来物以便被吞噬。
5. **Biocompatibility (生物相容性)**: The ability of a material to perform with an appropriate host response in a specific application, balancing the interaction between the material and tissues to achieve the desired function. （材料在特定应用中以适当的宿主反应执行功能的能力，平衡材料与组织间的相互作用以实现预期功能。）
   - **关联重点**：*ISO 10993* —— 国际公认的生物相容性评估标准标准。
6. **Cytotoxicity (细胞毒性)**: The quality of being toxic to cells; in biomaterials, it is often used as a first-tier screening test to determine if chemicals leaching from a material cause cell death or functional damage. （对细胞具有毒性的性质；在生物材料中，常作为第一级筛选测试，确定从材料中浸出的化学物质是否导致细胞死亡或功能损害。）
   - **关联重点**：*In vitro testing (体外测试)* —— 细胞毒性测试的主要形式。
7. **Mutagenicity (诱变性)**: The capacity of a substance to induce genetic mutations or damage DNA within a cell, which may lead to cancer or inherited defects. （物质诱导遗传突变或损害细胞内 DNA 的能力，这可能导致癌症或遗传缺陷。）
   - **关联重点**：*Ames Test (Ames 试验)* —— 常用的细菌诱变性检测方法。
8. **Biofilm (生物膜)**: A structured community of microorganisms adherent to a surface and enclosed in a self-produced polymeric matrix, which protects bacteria from antibiotics and the host immune system. （粘附在表面的微生物结构化群体，包裹在自产的聚合物基质中，保护细菌免受抗生素和宿主免疫系统的攻击。）
   - **关联重点**：*Anti-fouling (防污)* —— 如 PEG 改性，旨在防止生物膜形成。

### 第三部分：判断题 (True & False Questions - 20题)

1. **Protein adsorption occurs within seconds of implantation and precedes cell attachment.** **蛋白吸附在植入后几秒钟内发生，并先于细胞附着。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Proteins are much smaller and faster than cells, so they always reach the surface first. (中) 蛋白质比细胞小得多且移动更快，因此总是先到达表面。
2. **Acute inflammation typically lasts for several months.** **急性炎症通常持续数月。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Acute inflammation is a short-term response (hours to days). Long-term inflammation is called chronic. (中) 急性炎症是短期反应（几小时到几天）。长期炎症被称为慢性炎症。
3. **The classical pathway of complement activation requires antibodies.** **补体激活的经典途径需要抗体。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) The classical pathway is triggered by antibody-antigen complexes. (中) 经典途径由抗体-抗体复合物触发。
4. **Fibrotic encapsulation is a way for the body to integrate the material into the original tissue.** **纤维包囊是身体将材料整合到原始组织中的一种方式。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Fibrotic encapsulation is a way to **isolate** (wall off) the foreign material, not integrate it. (中) 纤维包囊是**隔离**（围堵）外来材料的方式，而不是整合。
5. **A material can be considered "100% biocompatible" regardless of its application.** **无论应用场景如何，一种材料都可以被认为是“100% 生物相容”的。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Biocompatibility is application-specific; a material safe for skin might be toxic in the blood. (中) 生物相容性是特定于应用的；对皮肤安全的材料在血液中可能有毒。
6. **Platelets are cell fragments that play a key role in blood clotting.** **血小板是细胞碎片，在血液凝固中起关键作用。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) They adhere to exposed collagen and release factors to form a plug. (中) 它们粘附在暴露的胶原蛋白上并释放因子形成血栓。
7. **In vitro tests are always sufficient to predict the full host response.** **体外测试总是足以预测完整的宿主反应。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) In vitro tests lack the complexity of systemic interactions found in living organisms (In vivo). (中) 体外测试缺乏活体（体内）中存在的系统性相互作用的复杂性。
8. **Leachable substances can cause systemic toxicity even if the implant is localized.** **即使植入物是局部的，可浸提物质也可能引起全身毒性。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Chemicals can enter the bloodstream and travel to organs like the liver or kidneys. (中) 化学物质可以进入血液并流向肝脏或肾脏等器官。
9. **The alternative pathway of complement is often activated by the hydroxyl groups on polymer surfaces.** **补体旁路途径通常由聚合物表面的羟基激活。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) This is a common mechanism for biomaterial-induced immune response. (中) 这是生物材料诱导免疫反应的常见机制。
10. **Chronic inflammation is characterized by the presence of neutrophils.** **慢性炎症的特征是存在中性粒细胞。**
    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Neutrophils are signs of acute inflammation; chronic inflammation involves macrophages and lymphocytes. (中) 中性粒细胞是急性炎症的征兆；慢性炎症涉及巨噬细胞和淋巴细胞。

    - **答案：True (正确)**

    - **解析：** (En) PEG's hydrophilic nature creates a barrier that repels proteins (antifouling). (中) PEG 的亲水性创造了一个排斥蛋白质的屏障（防污）。

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions - 5题)

**1. Briefly describe the temporal sequence of events that occur following the implantation of a biomaterial.** **简述生物材料植入后发生的事件的时间顺序。**

- **答案：** Immediately upon implantation, protein adsorption occurs (within nanoseconds). This is followed by acute inflammation (hours to days), where neutrophils migrate to the site. If the material persists, it transitions to chronic inflammation (weeks), involving macrophages and lymphocytes. Eventually, a foreign body response occurs, characterized by Foreign Body Giant Cells (FBGCs) and the formation of a fibrotic capsule around the material. （植入后立即发生蛋白吸附（纳秒内）。随后是急性炎症（数小时到数天），中性粒细胞迁移至该部位。如果材料持续存在，则转为慢性炎症（数周），涉及巨噬细胞和淋巴细胞。最终，发生异物反应，其特征是异物巨细胞 (FBGCs) 并在材料周围形成纤维包囊。）

**2. What is the difference between the Intrinsic and Extrinsic pathways of blood coagulation?** **血液凝固的内源性途径和外源性途径有什么区别？**

- **答案：** The Extrinsic pathway is triggered by Tissue Factor (TF) released from damaged tissues outside the blood vessel. The Intrinsic pathway (contact activation pathway) is triggered when Factor XII comes into contact with a negatively charged foreign surface, such as a biomaterial. Both pathways eventually meet at the common pathway to activate thrombin, which converts fibrinogen into a fibrin clot. （外源性途径由血管外受损组织释放的组织因子 (TF) 触发。内源性途径（接触激活途径）则是在因子 XII 与带负电荷的外来表面（如生物材料）接触时触发。两条途径最终在共同途径汇合，激活凝血酶，将纤维蛋白原转化为纤维蛋白凝块。）

**3. Explain why "Wear Debris" from an implant can lead to bone loss (osteolysis).** **解释为什么植入物的“磨损碎片”会导致骨流失（骨溶解）。**

- **答案：** Small particles of wear debris are phagocytosed by macrophages. Because the particles cannot be degraded, they trigger a persistent chronic inflammatory response. The macrophages release pro-inflammatory cytokines (like TNF-alpha) that activate osteoclasts (cells that break down bone). This leads to periprosthetic bone resorption, eventually causing the implant to loosen. （细小的磨损碎片被巨噬细胞吞噬。由于颗粒无法降解，它们会引发持续的慢性炎症反应。巨噬细胞释放促炎细胞因子（如 TNF-alpha），从而激活破骨细胞（分解骨骼的细胞）。这导致植入物周围的骨吸收，最终导致植入物松动。）

**4. Why is ISO 10993 evaluation categorized based on "Nature of Contact" and "Duration of Contact"?** **为什么 ISO 10993 的评估是基于“接触性质”和“接触持续时间”来分类的？**

- **答案：** This categorization ensures that testing is proportional to the risk. The nature of contact (e.g., skin vs. blood vs. bone) determines which biological systems are at risk. The duration of contact (Limited < 24h, Prolonged 24h-30d, Permanent > 30d) determines the potential for cumulative toxicity, sensitization, or chronic effects like carcinogenicity. （这种分类确保了测试与风险成正比。接触性质（例如皮肤 vs. 血液 vs. 骨骼）决定了哪些生物系统处于风险中。接触持续时间（有限接触 < 24h，长期接触 24h-30d，永久接触 > 30d）则决定了累积毒性、致敏或慢性效应（如致癌性）的可能性。）

**5. How do Foreign Body Giant Cells (FBGCs) contribute to the degradation of a biomaterial?** **异物巨细胞 (FBGCs) 是如何促进生物材料降解的？**

- **答案：** FBGCs form by the fusion of macrophages on the surface of large implants. They create a sealed microenvironment between the cell and the material surface. Within this space, they release high concentrations of Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) and degradative enzymes. This localized "chemical attack" can lead to oxidative or enzymatic degradation of the polymer or corrosion of the metal. （FBGCs 通过在大植入物表面的巨噬细胞融合形成。它们在细胞与材料表面之间创造了一个密封的微环境。在此空间内，它们释放高浓度的活性氧 (ROS) 和降解酶。这种局部的“化学攻击”会导致聚合物的氧化或酶促降解，或金属的腐蚀。）
