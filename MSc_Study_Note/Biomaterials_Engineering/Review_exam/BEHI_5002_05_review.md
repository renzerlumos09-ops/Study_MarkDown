## 选择题

### 第一部分：多选题 (Multiple Choice Questions - 40题)

**1. What does "Pharmacokinetics" (PK) describe?** **“药代动力学” (PK) 描述的是什么？**

- A. What the drug does to the body. (药物对身体的作用。)
- B. What the body does to the drug over time. (身体随时间对药物的作用。)
- C. The color and shape of the drug. (药物的颜色和形状。)
- D. The cost of manufacturing the drug. (制造药物的成本。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) PK covers Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion (ADME). (中) PK 涵盖吸收、分布、代谢和排泄 (ADME)。

**2. What is "Pharmacodynamics" (PD)?** **什么是“药效学” (PD)？**

- A. The study of drug excretion. (药物排泄的研究。)
- B. The study of the biochemical and physiological effects of drugs on the body. (药物对身体的生化和生理效应的研究。)
- C. The process of drug dissolution. (药物溶解的过程。)
- D. How the drug is packaged. (药物如何包装。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) PD focuses on the relationship between drug concentration and effect (what the drug does to the body). (中) PD 侧重于药物浓度与效应之间的关系（药物对身体的作用）。

**3. Which of the following is NOT a part of the ADME process?** **以下哪项不属于 ADME 过程？**

- A. Absorption (吸收)
- B. Distribution (分布)
- C. Adhesion (粘附)
- D. Excretion (排泄)
- **答案：C**
- **解析：** (En) The four steps are Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion. (中) 四个步骤是吸收、分布、代谢和排泄。

**4. What is the most common mechanism for drug absorption?** **药物吸收最常见的机制是什么？**

- A. Active transport (主动运输)
- B. Passive diffusion (被动扩散)
- C. Endocytosis (胞吞作用)
- D. Facilitated diffusion (易化扩散)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Most drugs move from high to low concentration across membranes without energy. (中) 大多数药物跨膜从高浓度向低浓度移动，无需能量。

**5. "Bioavailability" refers to:** **“生物利用度”是指：**

- A. The total weight of the drug. (药物的总重量。)
- B. The fraction of an administered dose that reaches the systemic circulation unchanged. (给药剂量中以未改变形式到达体循环的比例。)
- C. How long the drug stays in the stomach. (药物在胃里停留多久。)
- D. The speed of drug expiration. (药物过期的速度。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) IV administration has 100% bioavailability. (中) 静脉给药具有 100% 的生物利用度。

**6. What is the "Therapeutic Window"?** **什么是“治疗窗口”？**

- A. The time it takes to see a doctor. (看医生所需的时间。)
- B. The range between the Minimum Effective Concentration (MEC) and the Minimum Toxic Concentration (MTC). (最低有效浓度 (MEC) 和最低中毒浓度 (MTC) 之间的范围。)
- C. The physical size of the pill. (药丸的物理大小。)
- D. The period when the drug is on sale. (药物打折销售的期间。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Maintaining drug levels within this window ensures efficacy without toxicity. (中) 将药物水平保持在此窗口内可确保疗效且无毒性。

**7. Why is "Controlled Release" better than "Conventional Release"?** **为什么“受控释放”优于“常规释放”？**

- A. It is always cheaper. (它总是更便宜。)
- B. It reduces the frequency of dosing and maintains steady drug levels within the therapeutic window. (它减少了给药频率，并将药物水平稳定维持在治疗窗口内。)
- C. It makes the drug work instantly. (它使药物立即起效。)
- D. It uses no polymers. (它不使用聚合物。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Conventional release often causes "peaks" (toxicity) and "valleys" (ineffectiveness). (中) 常规释放经常导致“峰值”（毒性）和“谷值”（无效）。

**8. "Zero-order release" means the release rate is:** **“零级释放”意味着释放速率是：**

- A. Proportional to the remaining drug. (与剩余药物成正比。)
- B. Constant over time. (随时间恒定。)
- C. Decreasing exponentially. (呈指数级下降。)
- D. Random. (随机的。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) The amount released per unit time is independent of concentration. (中) 单位时间内释放的量与浓度无关。

**9. "First-order release" occurs when the rate is:** **“一级释放”发生在速率：**

- A. Constant. (恒定时。)
- B. Proportional to the amount of drug remaining in the device. (与设备中剩余的药物量成正比时。)
- C. Always zero. (始终为零时。)
- D. Faster as time goes on. (随时间推移变快时。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) As drug concentration drops, the release rate also drops. (中) 随着药物浓度下降，释放速率也随之下降。

**10. Fick’s First Law of Diffusion states that the flux (J) is proportional to:** **菲克第一扩散定律指出，通量 (J) 与以下哪项成正比：**

- A. The color gradient. (颜色梯度。)
- B. The concentration gradient (dC/dx). (浓度梯度 (dC/dx)。)
- C. The temperature. (温度。)
- D. The weight of the polymer. (聚合物的重量。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Molecules move from high concentration to low concentration. (中) 分子从高浓度向低浓度移动。

**11. In a "Reservoir System," the drug is:** **在“储库型系统”中，药物：**

- A. Uniformly mixed with the polymer. (与聚合物均匀混合。)
- B. Contained in a core surrounded by a semi-permeable membrane. (包含在由半透膜包裹的核心中。)
- C. Only on the surface. (仅在表面。)
- D. Chemically bonded to the polymer. (与聚合物化学结合。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) The membrane controls the diffusion rate. (中) 膜控制扩散速率。**[补充考点]**：如果核心保持饱和，可以实现零级释放。

**12. In a "Matrix System," the drug is:** **在“基质型系统”中，药物：**

- A. In a liquid core. (在液体核心中。)
- B. Uniformly dispersed or dissolved throughout the polymer. (均匀分散或溶解在整个聚合物中。)
- C. Attached to the outside of the pill only. (仅附着在药丸外部。)
- D. Not present. (不存在。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) The drug must diffuse through the polymer network to be released. (中) 药物必须通过聚合物网络扩散才能释放。

**13. The "Higuchi Equation" describes drug release from:** **“Higuchi 方程”描述了以下哪项的药物释放：**

- A. Liquid injections. (液体注射。)
- B. An ointment or a solid dosage form (Matrix systems). (软膏或固体剂型（基质系统）。)
- C. Gas bubbles. (气泡。)
- D. Pure water. (纯水。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) It relates the amount released to the square root of time (t^1/2). (中) 它将释放量与时间的平方根 (t^1/2) 联系起来。

**14. What does the "Korsmeyer-Peppas model" (Mt/M∞ = k\*t^n) help determine?** **Korsmeyer-Peppas 模型 (Mt/M∞ = k\*t^n) 有助于确定什么？**

- A. The price of the drug. (药物的价格。)
- B. The mechanism of drug release (based on the exponent 'n'). (基于指数 'n' 的药物释放机制。)
- C. The color of the polymer. (聚合物的颜色。)
- D. The patient's age. (患者的年龄。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) 'n' indicates if it's Fickian diffusion, anomalous transport, or Case II transport. (中) 'n' 指示它是菲克扩散、反常运输还是 Case II 运输。

**15. If n = 0.5 in a cylindrical system, the release mechanism is:** **在圆柱体系统中，如果 n = 0.5，释放机制是：**

- **答案：B**

**18. What is the "Glass Transition Temperature" (Tg)?** **什么是“玻璃化转变温度” (Tg)？**

- A. The temperature where glass melts. (玻璃熔化的温度。)

## 名词解释

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Pharmacokinetics (PK) (药代动力学)**:

   The study of what the body does to the drug, encompassing the processes of Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion (ADME).

   （研究机体对药物的作用，包括吸收、分布、代谢和排泄 (ADME) 的过程。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Bioavailability (生物利用度)* —— 药物到达体循环的速率和程度。

2. **Therapeutic Window (治疗窗口)**:

   The range of drug concentration between the Minimum Effective Concentration (MEC) and the Minimum Toxic Concentration (MTC).

   （药物浓度在最低有效浓度 (MEC) 和最低中毒浓度 (MTC) 之间的范围。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Steady State (稳态)* —— 给药速度与消除速度达到动态平衡的状态。

3. **Zero-Order Release (零级释放)**:

   A drug release profile where the rate of release is constant over time and independent of the remaining drug concentration.

   （一种药物释放曲线，其释放速率随时间恒定，且与剩余药物浓度无关。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Constant Flux (恒定通量)* —— 实现零级释放的物理基础。

4. **Fick’s First Law of Diffusion (菲克第一扩散定律)**:

   A mathematical relationship stating that the diffusion flux (J) is proportional to the concentration gradient (dC/dx).

   （一种数学关系，指出扩散通量 (J) 与浓度梯度 (dC/dx) 成正比。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Diffusion Coefficient (D, 扩散系数)* —— 衡量物质扩散能力的比例常数。

5. **Matrix System (基质型系统)**:

   A delivery system where the drug is uniformly dissolved or dispersed throughout a solid polymer network.

   （一种递送系统，药物在其中均匀地溶解或分散在整个固体聚合物网络中。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Higuchi Equation (Higuchi方程)* —— 描述基质系统释放的经典数学模型。

6. **Glass Transition Temperature (Tg) (玻璃化转变温度)**:

   The temperature at which a polymer transitions from a hard, glassy state to a soft, rubbery state, significantly increasing molecular mobility.

   （聚合物从坚硬的玻璃态转变为柔软的橡胶态的温度，此时分子流动性显著增加。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Polymer Relaxation (聚合物松弛)* —— 伴随 Tg 发生的结构变化。

7. **Swelling Front (溶胀前沿)**:

   The moving boundary that separates the dry (glassy) polymer core from the hydrated (rubbery) polymer layer as water penetrates.

   （随着水分渗透，将干燥（玻璃态）聚合物核心与水化（橡胶态）聚合物层分开的移动边界。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Anomalous Transport (反常运输)* —— 扩散速度与溶胀速度相当时的情形。

8. **Surface Erosion (表面侵蚀)**:

   A degradation process where the polymer breaks down only at the exterior surface, allowing the device to shrink in size while maintaining its bulk structural integrity.

   （一种降解过程，聚合物仅在外部表面分解，使设备尺寸缩小但保持其整体结构完整性。）

   - **关联重要名词**：*Bulk Erosion (整体侵蚀)* —— 水分进入整个内部导致整体降解。

### 第三部分：判断题 (True & False Questions - 20题)

1. **Passive diffusion is the most common mechanism for drug absorption.**

   **被动扩散是药物吸收最常见的机制。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Most drugs cross membranes from high to low concentration without requiring energy. (中) 大多数药物跨膜从高浓度向低浓度移动，不需要能量。

2. **In first-order kinetics, the drug release rate remains constant over time.**

   **在一级动力学中，药物释放速率随时间保持恒定。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) In first-order, the rate depends on concentration and decreases as the drug is depleted. (中) 在一级动力学中，速率取决于浓度，并随着药物耗尽而降低。

3. **The blood-brain barrier (BBB) is easily crossed by hydrophilic drugs.**

   **亲水性药物很容易跨越血脑屏障 (BBB)。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) The BBB is a highly selective barrier; hydrophilic and large molecules struggle to cross without transporters. (中) BBB 是高度选择性的屏障；亲水和大分子在没有转运体的情况下很难跨越。

4. **A "Reservoir System" can achieve zero-order release if the drug core remains saturated.**

   **如果药物核心保持饱和，“储库型系统”可以实现零级释放。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) A constant concentration gradient across the membrane results in a constant release rate. (中) 跨膜的恒定浓度梯度导致恒定的释放速率。

5. **Higuchi Equation states that drug release is proportional to time (t).**

   **Higuchi 方程指出药物释放与时间 (t) 成正比。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) The Higuchi model states release is proportional to the **square root of time ($\sqrt{t}$)**. (中) Higuchi 模型指出释放量与**时间的平方根 ($\sqrt{t}$)** 成正比。

6. **The "Burst Effect" is always a desirable feature to reach therapeutic levels quickly.**

   **“突释效应”总是一个理想的特征，可以快速达到治疗水平。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) While it can be useful, it often carries a risk of toxicity if levels exceed the MTC. (中) 虽然有时有用，但如果浓度超过 MTC，通常带有中毒风险。

7. **In the Korsmeyer-Peppas model, n = 1.0 for a slab indicates Fickian diffusion.**

   **在 Korsmeyer-Peppas 模型中，片状结构的 n = 1.0 表示菲克扩散。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) For a slab, n = 0.5 is Fickian; n = 1.0 is Zero-order (Case II transport). (中) 对于片状，n = 0.5 是菲克扩散；n = 1.0 是零级释放（Case II 运输）。

8. **"Bioavailability" is 100% for drugs administered intravenously (IV).**

   **静脉注射 (IV) 给药的药物“生物利用度”为 100%。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) IV injection bypasses absorption barriers and goes directly into systemic circulation. (中) 静脉注射绕过了吸收屏障，直接进入体循环。

9. **A polymer in its rubbery state (T > Tg) has much higher drug diffusivity than in its glassy state.**

   **处于橡胶态 (T > Tg) 的聚合物比处于玻璃态时具有更高的药物扩散率。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Increased chain mobility in the rubbery state allows drug molecules to move more freely. (中) 橡胶态中增加的链流动性允许药物分子更自由地移动。

10. **Bulk erosion typically maintains the constant external dimensions of the drug delivery device.**

    **整体侵蚀通常会保持药物递送设备的外部尺寸恒定。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) Unlike surface erosion, bulk erosion involves degradation throughout the volume, often leaving the size unchanged until collapse. (中) 与表面侵蚀不同，整体侵蚀涉及整个体积的降解，通常在崩溃前保持尺寸不变。

11. **The "Diffusion Front" moves from the outside of the tablet towards the center.**

    **“扩散前沿”从药片的外部向中心移动。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) As water moves in, the dissolved drug follows the gradient out, with the boundary of dissolved/undissolved drug moving inward. (中) 随着水进入，溶解的药物随梯度流出，溶解/未溶解药物的边界向内移动。

12. **The partition coefficient (K) measures the drug's solubility limit in water.**

    **分配系数 (K) 测量的是药物在水中的溶解度极限。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) K is the ratio of drug concentration in the polymer versus the release medium at equilibrium. (中) K 是平衡时聚合物中的药物浓度与释放介质中的浓度之比。

13. **Surface-eroding polymers are ideal for achieving zero-order release.**

    **表面侵蚀聚合物是实现零级释放的理想选择。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) If the surface area remains constant, the release rate (linked to erosion) stays constant. (中) 如果表面积保持恒定，释放速率（与侵蚀相关）也将保持恒定。

14. **Metabolism always makes a drug more active.**

    **代谢总是使药物变得更具活性。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Most metabolism (detoxification) makes drugs less active or inactive; only pro-drugs become more active. (中) 大多数代谢（解毒）会降低药物活性或使其失活；只有“前药”会变得更有活性。

15. **Lag time is the time required for a drug to reach its maximum peak concentration.**

    **滞后时间是药物达到其最高峰浓度所需的时间。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Lag time is the initial period where no drug is released (e.g., waiting for the polymer to swell). (中) 滞后时间是最初没有药物释放的时间段（例如，等待聚合物溶胀）。

16. **Elimination half-life (t1/2) is independent of the drug dose in first-order elimination.**

    **在一级消除中，消除半衰期 (t1/2) 与药物剂量无关。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) In first-order kinetics, the time to reduce the concentration by half is constant. (中) 在一级动力学中，浓度降低一半所需的时间是恒定的。

17. **Case II transport is driven primarily by the diffusion gradient.**

    **Case II 运输主要由扩散梯度驱动。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Case II transport is driven by polymer relaxation (swelling), not just diffusion. (中) Case II 运输由聚合物松弛（溶胀）驱动，而不仅仅是扩散。

18. **The presence of salts can affect the swelling behavior of ionic hydrogels.**

    **盐的存在会影响离子型水凝胶的溶胀行为。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) Ion concentration affects osmotic pressure and the degree of swelling in charged polymer networks. (中) 离子浓度会影响渗透压和带电聚合物网络的溶胀程度。

19. **Fick's Second Law accounts for concentration changes over time.**

    **菲克第二定律考虑了浓度随时间的变化。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) It relates the rate of change of concentration to the spatial gradient ($\partial C/\partial t = D \nabla^2 C$). (中) 它将浓度随时间的变化率与空间梯度联系起来。

20. **Controlled release systems are only used for oral drug delivery.**

    **受控释放系统仅用于口服给药。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) They are used in implants, transdermal patches, injections, and more. (中) 它们还用于植入物、透皮贴剂、注射等。

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions - 5题)

**1. Compare the plasma concentration profiles of conventional drug administration versus controlled drug release. Why is controlled release often preferred?**

**比较常规给药与受控药物释放的血浆浓度曲线。为什么受控释放通常更受青睐？**

- **答案：** Conventional administration (e.g., pills, injections) often results in fluctuating plasma levels with "peaks" that may exceed the Minimum Toxic Concentration (MTC) and "valleys" that fall below the Minimum Effective Concentration (MEC). Controlled release aims to maintain a steady-state concentration within the therapeutic window for an extended period. This reduces dosing frequency, improves patient compliance, and minimizes side effects.

  （常规给药（如药片、注射）通常会导致血浆水平波动，出现可能超过最低中毒浓度 (MTC) 的“峰值”和低于最低有效浓度 (MEC) 的“谷值”。受控释放旨在较长时间内将稳态浓度维持在治疗窗口内。这减少了给药频率，提高了患者依从性，并最大限度地减少了副作用。）

**2. Describe the physical meaning of the three moving fronts in a swelleble, water-soluble polymer tablet.**

**描述可溶胀、水溶性聚合物药片中三个移动前沿的物理含义。**

- **答案：**
  1. Swelling Front: The boundary between the dry glassy polymer and the hydrated rubbery polymer where polymer chain relaxation occurs. (溶胀前沿：干燥的玻璃态聚合物与发生链松弛的水化橡胶态聚合物之间的边界。)
  2. Diffusion Front: The boundary separating the region containing only dissolved drug from the region containing both dissolved and undissolved drug. (扩散前沿：将仅含有溶解药物的区域与同时含有溶解和未溶解药物的区域分开的边界。)
  3. Erosion Front: The outermost surface of the matrix that is dissolving into the bulk fluid. (侵蚀前沿：基质最外层溶解进入主体流体的表面。)

**3. Explain Fick’s First Law and its significance in diffusion-controlled drug delivery systems.**

**解释菲克第一定律及其在扩散控制药物递送系统中的意义。**

- **答案：** Fick’s First Law is expressed as $J = -D \frac{dc}{dx}$, where $J$ is the diffusion flux, $D$ is the diffusion coefficient, and $\frac{dc}{dx}$ is the concentration gradient. It signifies that drug molecules move from high concentration to low concentration. In DDS, it allows engineers to predict and control the release rate by adjusting the membrane thickness, the drug concentration, or the choice of polymer (which affects $D$).

  （菲克第一定律表达式为 $J = -D \frac{dc}{dx}$，其中 $J$ 是扩散通量，$D$ 是扩散系数，$\frac{dc}{dx}$ 是浓度梯度。它表示药物分子从高浓度向低浓度移动。在 DDS 中，它允许工程师通过调整膜厚度、药物浓度或聚合物的选择（影响 $D$）来预测和控制释放速率。）

**4. What is the "Glass Transition Temperature" (Tg) and how does it affect the drug release rate from a polymer matrix?**

**什么是“玻璃化转变温度” (Tg)？它如何影响聚合物基质的药物释放速率？**

- **答案：** Tg is the temperature at which a polymer shifts from a hard/glassy state to a soft/rubbery state. When $T > Tg$, the mobility of polymer chains increases significantly. This results in an increase in the drug diffusion coefficient (D), because the "gaps" between moving polymer chains allow drug molecules to pass through more easily. In the glassy state ($T < Tg$), diffusion is often extremely slow or negligible.

  （Tg 是聚合物从硬/玻璃态转变为软/橡胶态的温度。当 $T > Tg$ 时，聚合物链的流动性显著增加。这导致药物扩散系数 (D) 增加，因为移动的聚合物链之间的“间隙”使药物分子更容易通过。在玻璃态 ($T < Tg$) 下，扩散通常极其缓慢或可以忽略不计。）

**5. Distinguish between "Reservoir Systems" and "Matrix Systems" in terms of structure and release kinetics.**

**从结构和释放动力学方面区分“储库型系统”和“基质型系统”。**

- **答案：** In a Reservoir System, a drug core is surrounded by a rate-controlling membrane; it can achieve zero-order release if the core stays saturated. In a Matrix System, the drug is uniformly dispersed throughout the polymer; the release rate typically decreases over time (following the Higuchi model or $\sqrt{t}$ kinetics) as the diffusion distance for the drug inside the matrix increases.

  （在储库型系统中，药物核心被一层速率控制膜包裹；如果核心保持饱和，它可以实现零级释放。在基质型系统中，药物均匀分散在整个聚合物中；释放速率通常随时间降低（遵循 Higuchi 模型或 $\sqrt{t}$ 动力学），因为基质内部药物的扩散距离随之增加。）
