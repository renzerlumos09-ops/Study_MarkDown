**2. Which class of artificial organs can totally and permanently replace failed organs?** **哪一类人工器官可以完全且永久地替换衰竭器官？**

**6. In mass transport, what does "Convection" refer to?** **在质量传递中，“对流”是指什么？**

- A. Movement due to concentration gradients. (由于浓度梯度引起的运动。)

**7. "Diffusion" in artificial organs is driven by:** **人工器官中的“扩散”是由什么驱动的？** * A. Pressure gradient. (压力梯度。)

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Mass Transport (质量传递)**: The movement of molecules (such as oxygen, nutrients, or metabolic wastes) through diffusion and convection to ensure the survival and function of cells within an artificial organ. （分子（如氧气、营养物质或代谢废物）通过扩散和对流进行的移动，旨在确保人工器官内细胞的存活与功能。）

2. **Decellularization (脱细胞化)**: A process that removes all cellular components from a donor organ while preserving the structural and chemical integrity of the extracellular matrix (ECM). （从供体器官中去除所有细胞成分，同时保留细胞外基质 (ECM) 的结构和化学完整性的过程。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Scaffold (支架)* —— 脱细胞后留下的天然支撑结构。
3. **Bio-ink (生物墨水)**: A mixture of living cells and biomaterials (typically hydrogels) used in 3D bioprinting to create complex tissue structures layer by layer. （活细胞与生物材料（通常为水凝胶）的混合物，用于 3D 生物打印以逐层创建复杂的组织结构。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*3D Bioprinting (3D 生物打印)* —— 制造生物人工器官的核心技术。
4. **Biocompatibility (生物相容性)**: The ability of a biomaterial to perform its intended function without causing an undesirable local or systemic response in the host. （生物材料在执行其预期功能的同时，不会在宿主体内引起不良的局部或全身反应的能力。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Immune Protection (免疫保护)* —— 人工器官长期存活的关键。
5. **Nitinol (镍钛诺)**: A shape-memory alloy known for its excellent biocompatibility and superelasticity, often used as a stent material in artificial organs to provide structural support. （一种形状记忆合金，以其优异的生物相容性和超弹性著称，常在人工器官中用作支架材料以提供结构支撑。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Stent (支架/内支架)* —— 维持管道通畅的机械部件。
6. **Encapsulation (封装)**: A technique to enclose living cells within a semi-permeable membrane that allows the transport of nutrients and waste but blocks immune cells and antibodies. （将活细胞包裹在半透膜内的技术，允许营养物质和废物传输，但阻挡免疫细胞和抗体。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Alginate (海藻酸钠)* —— 常用的封装水凝胶材料。
7. **Extracellular Matrix (ECM) (细胞外基质)**: The non-cellular component present within all tissues that provides essential physical scaffolding and biochemical cues for cell attachment and tissue morphogenesis. （存在于所有组织中的非细胞成分，为细胞附着和组织形态发生提供必要的物理支架和生化信号。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Regeneration (再生)* —— 利用 ECM 引导新组织生长的过程。
8. **Bottom-up Engineering (自下而上工程)**: A fabrication approach that assembles small functional units or cell-laden layers to build complex 3D structures, such as layer-by-layer 3D bioprinting. （一种组装小型功能单元或载细胞层来构建复杂 3D 结构的制造方法，例如逐层 3D 生物打印。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Inside-out Engineering (由内而外工程)* —— 从核心向外逐层增加的制造技术。

## 简答

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions)

**1. Describe the three classes of artificial organs and their respective capabilities in treating organ failure.** **描述三类人工器官及其在治疗器官衰竭方面的各自能力。**

- **答案：**
  - **Mechanical (机械):** Made of inanimate metals and polymers (e.g., dialysis). Can only partially and temporarily replace functions. (由无生命金属和聚合物制成；只能部分且临时地替代功能。)
  - **Biomechanical (生物机械):** Combines mechanical components with some biological elements. Also limited to temporary or partial repair. (结合了机械组件和生物元素；同样局限于临时或部分修复。)
  - **Biological (Bioartificial) (生物/生物人工):** Uses cells and biomaterials. Aim to totally and permanently replace and cure failed organs. (使用细胞和生物材料；旨在完全且永久地替代并治愈衰竭器官。)

**2. Explain the difference between "Diffusion" and "Convection" in the context of mass transport in artificial organs.** **在人工器官质量传递的背景下，解释“扩散”与“对流”的区别。**

- **答案：**
  - **Diffusion (扩散):** Movement of molecules driven by a concentration gradient (from high to low concentration). (由浓度梯度驱动的分子运动。)
  - **Convection (对流):** Bulk flow of fluid driven by pressure differences or fluid velocity. (由压力差或流体流速驱动的流体整体流动。)
  - **Importance (重要性):** Both are critical for nutrient delivery and waste removal in scaffolds. (两者对于支架内的营养输送和废物清除都至关重要。)

**3. What is the "Diffusion Limit" and why is "Vascularization" essential for large bioartificial organs?** **什么是“扩散限制”？为什么“血管化”对大型生物人工器官至关重要？**

- **答案：** Oxygen and nutrients can only diffuse effectively over a short distance (approx. 100-200 μm). For larger tissues, cells in the center will die without a blood supply. (氧气和营养物质只能在短距离（约100-200微米）内有效扩散。) Vascularization (forming blood vessels) is essential to provide convective transport and ensure nutrient access to all parts of the 3D structure. (血管化（形成血管）对于提供对流传输并确保3D结构各部分获得营养供应至关重要。)

**4. Compare "Bottom-up" and "Outside-in" engineering technologies in organ fabrication.** **比较器官制造中的“自下而上”与“由外而内”工程技术。**

- **答案：**
  - **Bottom-up (自下而上):** Assembles cells and materials in a layer-by-layer deposition manner (e.g., 3D Bioprinting) to build complex architectures. (以逐层沉积的方式组装细胞和材料，以构建复杂的结构。)
  - **Outside-in (由外而内):** Involves attaching cells onto a pre-existing scaffold, such as decellularized organ regeneration. (涉及将细胞附着在预先存在的支架上，例如脱细胞器官再生。)

**5. How does a "Bioartificial Pancreas" use encapsulation to protect islet cells?** **生物人工胰腺如何利用封装技术保护胰岛细胞？**

- **答案：** It uses a semi-permeable membrane (often hydrogels like Alginate). This membrane allows small molecules like glucose and insulin to pass through freely, while blocking large immune components like antibodies and immune cells from attacking the foreign islets. (它使用一种半透膜（通常是海藻酸钠等水凝胶）。这种膜允许小分子（如葡萄糖和胰岛素）自由通过，同时阻挡大型免疫成分（如抗体和免疫细胞）攻击外源胰岛。)
