**聚合物的化学/生化降解（水解、氧化）、陶瓷的溶蚀、金属的腐蚀以及病理性钙化**。

## 选择

### 第一部分：多选题 (Multiple Choice Questions - 40题)

**1. What is the definition of "Biodegradation"?**

**“生物降解”的定义是什么？**

- A. Physical wear of materials due to friction. (由于摩擦导致的材料物理磨损。)
- B. Chemical breakdown of materials by the action of living organisms leading to changes in physical properties. (通过生物体的作用使材料发生化学分解，导致物理性质改变。)
- C. The process of material melting at body temperature. (材料在体温下熔化的过程。)
- D. Adding biological cells to a material surface. (在材料表面添加生物细胞。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) It involves chemical changes mediated by the biological environment. (中) 它涉及由生物环境介导的化学变化。

**2. Which two main chemical mechanisms drive the degradation of synthetic polymers?**

**驱动合成聚合物降解的两个主要化学机制是什么？**

- A. Melting and Freezing. (熔化与冷冻。)
- B. Hydrolysis and Oxidation. (水解与氧化。)
- C. Carbonization and Ionization. (炭化与电离。)
- D. Sublimation and Condensation. (升华与冷凝。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Hydrolysis involves water cleaving bonds, while oxidation involves reactive oxygen species (ROS). (中) 水解涉及水断裂化学键，而氧化涉及活性氧 (ROS)。

**3. "Hydrolysis" is the cleavage of chemical bonds by the addition of:**

**“水解”是通过添加以下哪种物质来断裂化学键：**

- A. Oxygen (氧气)
- B. Hydrogen gas (氢气)
- C. Water (水)
- D. Carbon dioxide (二氧化碳)
- **答案：C**
- **解析：** (En) It is the most common degradation mechanism for polymers containing ester or amide groups. (中) 这是含有酯基或酰胺基的聚合物最常见的降解机制。

**4. Which factor significantly increases the rate of polymer hydrolysis?**

**哪个因素会显著增加聚合物的水解速率？**

- A. High crystallinity. (高结晶度。)
- B. Presence of polar groups and high hydrophilicity. (存在极性基团和高亲水性。)
- C. Increasing the molecular weight. (增加分子量。)
- D. Reducing the surface area. (减少表面积。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Hydrophilic materials attract water, accelerating the bond-breaking process. (中) 亲水性材料吸引水，加速化学键断裂。**[补充考点]**：低结晶度（无定形区）也比高结晶区更容易水解。

**5. What is "Bulk Erosion"?**

**什么是“整体侵蚀”？**

- A. Degradation occurs only on the material surface. (降解仅发生在材料表面。)
- B. Water penetrates the entire material, causing degradation throughout the interior. (水渗透整个材料，导致内部各处发生降解。)
- C. The material is eaten by bacteria. (材料被细菌吃掉。)
- D. Only the edges of the material dissolve. (只有材料的边缘溶解。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) In bulk erosion, the rate of water penetration is faster than the rate of polymer breakdown. (中) 在整体侵蚀中，水的渗透速率快于聚合物的分解速率。

**6. "Surface Erosion" is characterized by:**

**“表面侵蚀”的特征是：**

- A. The device maintaining its shape while getting smaller. (设备在变小的同时保持其形状。)
- B. A sudden collapse of the entire structure. (整个结构突然崩塌。)
- C. Water entering the core of the material quickly. (水快速进入材料核心。)
- D. No change in the material weight. (材料重量没有变化。)
- **答案：A**
- **解析：** (En) The degradation rate is faster than the water penetration rate, so only the surface "peels" off. (中) 降解速率快于水渗透速率，因此只有表面“剥落”。

**7. Which polymer is known for undergoing surface erosion?**

**哪种聚合物以发生表面侵蚀而闻名？**

- A. PLA (聚乳酸)
- B. PGA (聚甘醇酸)
- C. Polyanhydrides (聚酸酐)
- D. Polyethylene (聚乙烯)
- **答案：C**
- **解析：** (En) Polyanhydrides are highly reactive with water but hydrophobic enough to prevent deep penetration, leading to surface erosion. (中) 聚酸酐与水反应活性高但具有足够的疏水性以防止深度渗透，从而导致表面侵蚀。

**8. Oxidative degradation is primarily mediated by which cell type?**

**氧化降解主要由哪种细胞介导？**

- A. Red blood cells. (红细胞。)
- B. Neutrophils and Macrophages. (中性粒细胞和巨噬细胞。)
- C. Bone cells (Osteoblasts). (骨细胞（成骨细胞）。)
- D. Nerve cells. (神经细胞。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) These inflammatory cells release reactive oxygen species (ROS) like $H_2O_2$ to attack the material. (中) 这些炎症细胞释放过氧化氢等活性氧 (ROS) 来攻击材料。

**9. What is "Stress Corrosion Cracking" (SCC)?**

**什么是“应力腐蚀开裂” (SCC)？**

- A. Cracking due to cold temperatures. (由于低温导致的开裂。)
- B. The combined effect of a corrosive environment and mechanical tensile stress. (腐蚀环境和机械拉伸应力的共同作用。)
- C. Rusting of a metal in a dry box. (干燥箱中金属生锈。)
- D. Cracking caused by light exposure. (光照引起的开裂。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) SCC can lead to unexpected brittle failure of ductile metals in the body. (中) SCC 可能导致体内韧性金属发生意外的脆性断裂。

**10. "Galvanic Corrosion" occurs when:**

**“电偶腐蚀”发生在：**

- A. One metal is heated. (一种金属被加热。)
- B. Two dissimilar metals are in electrical contact in an electrolyte (like body fluid). (两种不同的金属在电解质（如体液）中发生电接触。)
- C. A metal is coated with plastic. (金属表面涂有塑料。)
- D. A metal is submerged in pure water. (金属浸泡在纯水中。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) The less noble metal (anode) corrodes faster than it would alone. (中) 电位较低的金属（阳极）腐蚀速度比其单独存在时更快。

**11. Which process is responsible for the degradation of natural biomaterials like collagen?**

**哪种过程负责胶原蛋白等天然生物材料的降解？**

- A. Simple dissolution. (简单的溶解。)
- B. Enzymatic degradation (e.g., by collagenase). (酶促降解（例如通过胶原酶）。)

- D. Galvanic effect. (电偶效应。)
- **答案：B**

**12. What is "Pitting Corrosion"?**

**什么是“点蚀”？**

**13. "Crevice Corrosion" occurs in:**

**“缝隙腐蚀”发生在：**

**什么是“活性氧” (ROS)？**

## 名词解释

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Biodegradation (生物降解)**:

   A process of chemical breakdown of materials mediated by the action of living organisms (cells, enzymes, or biological fluids), leading to changes in physical properties.

   （由生物体作用（细胞、酶或生物体液）介导的材料化学分解过程，导致物理性质改变。）

   - **关联重点**：*Bioerosion (生物侵蚀)* —— 侧重于材料质量的物理流失。

2. **Hydrolysis (水解)**:

   A chemical reaction in which water molecules cleave chemical bonds (such as esters, amides, or anhydrides) in a polymer backbone, causing scission of the polymer chains.

   （一种化学反应，水分子断裂聚合物骨架中的化学键（如酯、酰胺或酸酐），导致聚合物链断裂。）

   - **关联重点**：*Crystallinity (结晶度)* —— 结晶区比无定形区更难发生水解。

3. **Bulk Erosion (整体侵蚀)**:

   A degradation mode where water penetration is faster than the rate of polymer breakdown, causing degradation to occur throughout the entire volume of the material.

   （一种降解模式，水的渗透速度快于聚合物分解速度，导致降解在材料的整个体积内发生。）

   - **关联重点**：*Sudden Failure (突然失效)* —— 整体侵蚀常导致强度骤降。

4. **Surface Erosion (表面侵蚀)**:

   A degradation mode where the rate of polymer breakdown is faster than the rate of water penetration, resulting in material loss only from the exterior surface.

   （一种降解模式，聚合物分解速度快于水渗透速度，导致材料损失仅从外部表面发生。）

   - **关联重点**：*Zero-order release (零级释放)* —— 表面侵蚀利于维持恒定的药物释放。

5. **Passivation (钝化)**:

   The formation of a stable, protective oxide film on a metal surface that acts as a barrier to hinder further electrochemical corrosion.

   （在金属表面形成一层稳定的保护性氧化膜，作为屏障阻碍进一步的电化学腐蚀。）

   - **关联重点**：*Pitting (点蚀)* —— 钝化膜局部破损会引发高度集中的点蚀。

6. **Galvanic Corrosion (电偶腐蚀)**:

   Corrosion that occurs when two dissimilar metals are in electrical contact in an electrolytic environment, causing the less noble metal (anode) to corrode rapidly.

   （当两种不同的金属在电解质环境中发生电接触时发生的腐蚀，导致电位较低的金属（阳极）迅速腐蚀。）

   - **关联重点**：*Standard Reduction Potential (标准电极电位)* —— 决定谁是阳极的依据。

7. **Stress Corrosion Cracking (SCC, 应力腐蚀开裂)**:

   The premature brittle failure of a metal resulting from the synergistic effect of a corrosive environment and tensile stress.

   （由于腐蚀环境和拉伸应力的协同作用导致金属发生的过早脆性失效。）

   - **关联重点**：*Ductile-to-Brittle (延性转脆性)* —— 是 SCC 最危险的特征。

8. **Dystrophic Calcification (变性钙化)**:

   The pathologic deposition of calcium salts in damaged or diseased tissues, occurring even when systemic blood calcium levels are normal.

   （在受损或患病组织中发生的病理性钙盐沉积，即使全身血钙水平正常也会发生。）

   - **关联重点**：*Metastatic Calcification (转移性钙化)* —— 伴随全身矿物质代谢紊乱（高血钙）。

### 第三部分：判断题 (True & False Questions - 20题)

1. **Enzymatic degradation is more specific and typically faster than simple chemical hydrolysis.**

   **酶促降解比简单的化学水解更具特异性，且通常更快。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Enzymes act as biological catalysts, lowering the activation energy for bond cleavage. (中) 酶作为生物催化剂，降低了断键所需的活化能。

2. **Hydrophobic polymers are more prone to rapid hydrolysis than hydrophilic polymers.**

   **疏水性聚合物比亲水性聚合物更容易发生快速水解。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Hydrophilic polymers attract water, which is necessary for the hydrolysis reaction. (中) 亲水性聚合物吸引水，而水是水解反应所必需的。

3. **In bulk erosion, the external dimensions of the implant remain constant for a long period while its internal strength decreases.**

   **在整体侵蚀中，植入物的外部尺寸在很长一段时间内保持不变，而其内部强度却在下降。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Degradation happens inside, so the size doesn't change until the material physically collapses. (中) 降解发生在内部，因此尺寸直到材料物理崩塌前都不会改变。

4. **Polyanhydrides typically undergo bulk erosion.**

   **聚酸酐通常发生整体侵蚀。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Polyanhydrides are the classic example of **surface erosion** due to high reactivity and limited water penetration. (中) 聚酸酐是由于高反应活性和有限的水渗透而发生**表面侵蚀**的典型例子。

5. **Metals do not degrade in the body because they are inorganic.**

   **金属在体内不会降解，因为它们是无机物。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Metals degrade through an electrochemical process called corrosion. (中) 金属通过称为腐蚀的电化学过程发生降解。

6. **Pitting corrosion is dangerous because it is highly localized and hard to detect.**

   **点蚀是危险的，因为它高度局部化且难以检测。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) It can lead to sudden structural failure by creating deep holes in a seemingly intact surface. (中) 它通过在看似完好的表面产生深孔，导致突然的结构失效。

7. **Crevice corrosion is driven by a concentration gradient of oxygen (oxygen depletion) in narrow gaps.**

   **缝隙腐蚀是由狭窄间隙中氧的浓度梯度（氧耗尽）驱动的。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Low oxygen in the crevice makes it anodic, while the outer surface becomes cathodic. (中) 缝隙中低氧使其成为阳极，而外部表面成为阴极。

8. **Metastatic calcification only occurs in individuals with normal mineral metabolism.**

   **转移性钙化仅发生在矿物质代谢正常的人身上。**

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) It occurs in patients with **deranged** mineral metabolism (high calcium/phosphate levels). (中) 它发生在矿物质代谢**紊乱**（高钙/磷水平）的患者身上。

9. **Passive films on metals like Titanium (Ti) are essential for their biocompatibility.**

   **钛 (Ti) 等金属表面的钝化膜对其生物相容性至关重要。**

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) The oxide layer prevents the release of potentially toxic metal ions into the body. (中) 氧化层防止潜在有毒的金属离子释放到体内。

10. **Increasing the cross-linking density of a polymer usually speeds up its degradation.**

    **增加聚合物的交联密度通常会加快其降解速度。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Cross-linking restricts chain mobility and water access, usually slowing down degradation. (中) 交联限制了链的流动性和水分进入，通常会减慢降解。

11. **Natural materials like collagen are primarily degraded by oxidation.**

    **胶原蛋白等天然材料主要通过氧化降解。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Natural materials are primarily degraded by specific enzymes (proteases/collagenases). (中) 天然材料主要由特异性酶（蛋白酶/胶原酶）降解。

12. **Pathologic calcification of heart valves can lead to clinical failure of the implant.**

    **心脏瓣膜的病理性钙化会导致植入物的临床失效。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) Calcification stiffens the valve leaflets, preventing them from opening/closing properly. (中) 钙化使瓣膜叶片变硬，防止其正常开启/关闭。

13. **High mechanical deformation can enhance the calcification of biomaterials.**

    **高度的机械变形可以增强生物材料的钙化。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) Mechanical stress can create sites or energy states that favor mineral deposition. (中) 机械应力会产生有利于矿物质沉积的位点或能量状态。

14. **Corrosion fatigue is the same as simple mechanical fatigue.**

    **腐蚀疲劳与简单的机械疲劳相同。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Corrosion fatigue is the result of cyclic loading **plus** a corrosive environment, which is much more damaging. (中) 腐蚀疲劳是循环载荷**加上**腐蚀环境的结果，其破坏性大得多。

15. **The body environment ($37^\circ C$, saline) is considered non-aggressive to materials.**

    **人体环境（$37^\circ C$，生理盐水）被认为对材料不具侵蚀性。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) It is surprisingly aggressive due to ions, proteins, and cells. (中) 由于离子、蛋白质和细胞的存在，它具有惊人的侵蚀性。

16. **Lowering the pH of the local environment (acidification) can accelerate polymer hydrolysis.**

    **降低局部环境的 pH 值（酸化）会加速聚合物水解。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) Many hydrolysis reactions are acid-catalyzed. (中) 许多水解反应是酸催化的。

17. **Foreign Body Giant Cells (FBGCs) are involved in the oxidative degradation of polymers.**

    **异物巨细胞 (FBGCs) 参与了聚合物的氧化降解。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) FBGCs release concentrated reactive oxygen species at the material interface. (中) FBGCs 在材料界面释放集中的活性氧。

18. **Ceramics are generally more resistant to corrosion than metals.**

    **陶瓷通常比金属更耐腐蚀。**

    - **答案：True (正确)**
    - **解析：** (En) Most ceramics are already in an oxidized state, making them chemically stable in water. (中) 大多数陶瓷已经处于氧化态，使其在水中化学性质稳定。

19. **Glutaraldehyde pretreatment of natural tissue can reduce calcification.**

    **天然组织的戊二醛预处理可以减少钙化。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Glutaraldehyde cross-linking actually **promotes** calcification; ethanol wash is used to reduce it. (中) 戊二醛交联实际上会**促进**钙化；乙醇冲洗才用于减少钙化。

20. **Biodegradation is always an unwanted effect in biomaterials engineering.**

    **生物降解在生物材料工程中总是不想要的效果。**

    - **答案：False (错误)**
    - **解析：** (En) Many materials (like scaffolds or sutures) are **designed** to biodegrade once their job is done. (中) 许多材料（如支架或缝线）被**设计**为在完成任务后生物降解。

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions - 5题)

**1. Explain the difference between Bulk Erosion and Surface Erosion. Why is surface erosion often preferred for drug delivery?**

**解释整体侵蚀和表面侵蚀的区别。为什么药物递送通常更倾向于表面侵蚀？**

- **答案：** Bulk erosion occurs when water penetrates the material faster than it can break down, causing degradation throughout the entire volume. Surface erosion occurs when the polymer breakdown is faster than water penetration, causing material loss only from the surface. Surface erosion is preferred for drug delivery because it allows for predictable, zero-order release kinetics as the drug release is directly proportional to the surface area of the eroding device.

  （整体侵蚀发生在水分渗透快于材料分解时，导致整个体积内发生降解。表面侵蚀发生在聚合物分解快于水分渗透时，导致仅从表面发生材料损失。药物递送更倾向于表面侵蚀，因为它允许可预测的零级释放动力学，因为药物释放与侵蚀设备的表面积成正比。）

**2. Describe the electrochemical process of metallic corrosion in the body. What are the two necessary reactions?**

**描述体内金属腐蚀的电化学过程。哪两个反应是必需的？**

- **答案：** Metallic corrosion is an electrochemical process occurring in an electrolyte (body fluid). It requires two simultaneous reactions: (1) Anodic reaction (Oxidation), where the metal dissolves into ions and releases electrons ($M \rightarrow M^{n+} + ne^-$), and (2) Cathodic reaction (Reduction), where the released electrons are consumed (e.g., by oxygen and water to form $OH^-$). The transfer of charge between these reactions leads to material degradation.

  （金属腐蚀是发生在电解质（体液）中的电化学过程。它需要两个同时进行的反应：(1) 阳极反应（氧化），金属溶解成离子并释放电子 ($M \rightarrow M^{n+} + ne^-$)；(2) 阴极反应（还原），释放的电子被消耗（例如由氧气和水形成 $OH^-$）。这些反应之间的电荷转移导致了材料降解。）

**3. What are the factors that influence the rate of polymer hydrolysis in a biological environment?**

**在生物环境中影响聚合物水解速率的因素有哪些？**

- **答案：** The rate of hydrolysis is influenced by: (1) Chemical bond stability (e.g., anhydrides > esters > amides), (2) Hydrophilicity (親水性), as water is required for the reaction, (3) Crystallinity, as tightly packed crystals resist water entry, (4) Surface area to volume ratio, and (5) Local pH and enzymes, which can catalyze the reaction.

  （水解速率受以下因素影响：(1) 化学键稳定性（例如：酸酐 > 酯 > 酰胺），(2) 亲水性，因为反应需要水，(3) 结晶度，因为紧密排列的晶体阻碍水分进入，(4) 表面积与体积比，以及 (5) 局部 pH 值和酶，它们可以催化反应。）

**4. Compare Dystrophic Calcification and Metastatic Calcification.**

**比较变性钙化和转移性钙化。**

- **答案：** Dystrophic calcification occurs in damaged or diseased tissues (like a calcified heart valve) while the patient has normal blood calcium levels. Metastatic calcification occurs in otherwise normal tissues due to systemic mineral metabolism disorders that cause abnormally high blood calcium or phosphate levels.

  （变性钙化发生在受损或患病组织中（如钙化的心脏瓣膜），而患者的血钙水平正常。转移性钙化则是由于全身矿物质代谢紊乱导致异常高血钙或高血磷，从而在原本正常的组织中发生钙盐沉积。）

**5. How does the body’s inflammatory response contribute to the degradation of polymers?**

**身体的炎症反应如何促进聚合物的降解？**

- **答案：** During the inflammatory response, cells like neutrophils and macrophages (and later foreign body giant cells) migrate to the implant site. These cells release Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS), such as hydrogen peroxide ($H_2O_2$), and proteolytic enzymes. ROS can cause oxidative degradation by attacking polymer chains, while enzymes can catalyze hydrolysis, both leading to structural weakening and erosion of the material.

  （在炎症反应期间，中性粒细胞和巨噬细胞（以及后来的异物巨细胞）迁移到植入部位。这些细胞释放活性氧 (ROS)（如过氧化氢 $H_2O_2$）和蛋白水解酶。ROS 可以通过攻击聚合物链引起氧化降解，而酶可以催化水解，两者都会导致材料结构变弱和侵蚀。）
