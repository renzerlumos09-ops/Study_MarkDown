### 第一部分：多选题 (Multiple Choice Questions - 40题)

**1. What is the definition of "Tissue"?** **“组织”的定义是什么？**

- A. A group of identical cells only. (仅指一组相同的细胞。)
- B. An assembly of cells and their extracellular matrix (ECM) that carry out a specific function. (细胞及其细胞外基质的集合体，共同执行特定功能。)
- C. Any synthetic material used in surgery. (手术中使用的任何合成材料。)
- D. The liquid part of the blood. (血液的液体部分。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Tissue is more than just cells; it includes the ECM which provides structural and biochemical support. (中) 组织不仅是细胞，还包括提供结构和生化支持的 ECM。

**2. What are the two main types of Extracellular Matrix (ECM)?** **细胞外基质 (ECM) 的两种主要类型是什么？**

- A. Soft and Hard matrix. (软基质和硬基质。)
- B. Interstitial matrix and Basement membrane. (间质基质和基底膜。)
- C. Collagen and Elastin. (胶原蛋白和弹性蛋白。)
- D. Liquid and Solid matrix. (液体基质和固体基质。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Interstitial matrix is found between cells; basement membrane exists underneath epithelial tissues. (中) 间质基质存在于细胞间；基底膜存在于上皮组织下方。

**3. Which molecule attaches cells to the ECM?** **哪种分子将细胞连接到 ECM 上？**

- A. Water (水)
- B. Fibronectin (纤连蛋白)
- C. Calcium (钙)
- D. Glucose (葡萄糖)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Fibronectin acts as a bridge, attaching to integrins in the cell membrane and ECM proteins. (中) 纤连蛋白充当桥梁，连接细胞膜中的整联蛋白和 ECM 蛋白。

**4. What are the three key elements (triad) of Tissue Engineering?** **组织工程的三要素（三联征）是什么？**

- A. Metal, Plastic, and Cells. (金属、塑料和细胞。)
- B. Scaffolds, Cells, and Signals (e.g., growth factors). (支架、细胞和信号（如生长因子）。)
- C. Doctors, Patients, and Hospitals. (医生、患者和医院。)
- D. Oxygen, Water, and Food. (氧气、水和食物。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) These three components work together to regenerate tissue. (中) 这三个组成部分共同作用以再生组织。

**5. What is the primary role of a "Scaffold" in tissue engineering?** **“支架”在组织工程中的主要作用是什么？**

- A. To permanently replace the organ. (永久替代器官。)
- B. To provide a temporary 3D template for cell attachment and tissue growth. (为细胞附着和组织生长提供临时的 3D 模板。)
- C. To kill bacteria. (杀死细菌。)
- D. To store blood. (储存血液。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Scaffolds mimic the natural ECM until new tissue is formed. (中) 在新组织形成之前，支架模拟天然 ECM。

**6. Which of the following is NOT a requirement for an ideal scaffold?** **以下哪项不是理想支架的要求？**

- A. Biocompatibility (生物相容性)
- B. High electrical conductivity (高导电性)
- C. Controlled biodegradability (可控的生物降解性)
- D. Suitable mechanical properties (合适的机械性能)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) While some tissues (like nerve/muscle) benefit from conductivity, it is not a general requirement for all scaffolds. (中) 虽然某些组织（如神经/肌肉）受益于导电性，但它不是所有支架的普遍要求。

**7. Why is "Porosity" important in scaffold design?** **为什么“孔隙率”在支架设计中很重要？**

- A. To make the scaffold look pretty. (为了让支架好看。)
- B. To allow cell migration, nutrient diffusion, and waste removal. (允许细胞迁移、营养扩散和废物清除。)
- C. To reduce the cost of materials. (降低材料成本。)
- D. To make the scaffold transparent. (使支架透明。)
- **答案：B**
- **解析：** (En) Interconnected pores are essential for mass transport and vascularization. (中) 连通的孔隙对于质量传递和血管化至关重要。

**8. What does "Biodegradability" mean for a scaffold?** **支架的“生物降解性”意味着什么？**

- A. It should last forever. (它应该永久存在。)

- D. It should dissolve in water instantly. (它应该立即溶于水。)

- **解析：** (En) The scaffold is temporary; it must disappear as the body repairs itself. (中) 支架是临时的；随着身体自我修复，它必须消失。

- **解析：** (En) Current engineered skin often lacks complex appendages. (中) 目前的工程皮肤通常缺乏复杂的附属器官。

## 名词解释

### 第二部分：名词解释 (Term Definitions - 8个)

1. **Extracellular Matrix (ECM) (细胞外基质)**: A complex network of macromolecules and minerals (such as collagen and proteoglycans) that provides structural scaffold and biochemical support to the surrounding cells. （由大分子和矿物质（如胶原蛋白和蛋白聚糖）构成的复杂网络，为周围细胞提供结构支架和生化支持。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Basement Membrane (基底膜)* —— 位于上皮组织下方的片状特殊 ECM。
2. **Scaffold (支架)**: A temporary 3D structure designed to mimic the natural ECM, providing a template for cell attachment, proliferation, and tissue regeneration before degrading. （一种旨在模拟天然 ECM 的临时 3D 结构，在降解前为细胞附着、增殖和组织再生提供模板。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Biodegradability (生物降解性)* —— 支架随新组织生长而消失的能力。
3. **Interstitial Matrix (间质基质)**: A type of ECM found between the intercellular spaces of various tissues, forming a porous gel-like structure that surrounds cells. （存在于各种组织细胞间隙中的一种 ECM，形成包裹细胞的多孔凝胶状结构。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Interstitial Fluid (组织间液)* —— 充满在间质基质中，为细胞提供营养。
4. **Electrospinning (电纺丝)**: A fabrication technique that uses electric force to draw charged threads of polymer solutions into micro- or nano-scale fibers, creating a mesh with high surface area. （一种利用电力将带电聚合物溶液拉成微米或纳米级纤维的制造技术，可创建具有高表面积的网格。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Nanofibers (纳米纤维)* —— 模拟天然胶原纤维尺寸的关键产物。
5. **Porosity (孔隙率)**: The measure of void spaces in a material, which must be highly interconnected in scaffolds to facilitate cell migration and nutrient transport. （材料中空隙空间的度量，在支架中必须高度连通，以促进细胞迁移和营养传输。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Surface Area-to-Volume Ratio (表面积体积比)* —— 高孔隙率带来的高比表面积有利于细胞附着。
6. **Solvent Casting and Particulate Leaching (溶剂铸造与颗粒浸出)**: A method of creating porous scaffolds by mixing polymer with salt or sugar particles, forming a structure, and then dissolving the particles to leave behind pores. （通过将聚合物与盐或糖颗粒混合成型，然后溶解颗粒留下孔隙来创建多孔支架的方法。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Porogen (致孔剂)* —— 如盐颗粒，决定了最终孔径的大小。
7. **Hydrogel (水凝胶)**: A network of cross-linked polymer chains that are highly hydrophilic and can hold large amounts of water, resembling the softness and hydration of natural soft tissues. （高度亲水的交联聚合物链网络，可吸收大量水分，模拟了天然软组织的柔软度和含水量。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Swelling Ratio (溶胀率)* —— 水凝胶吸收水分后的体积变化特性。
8. **Biomimetic (仿生)**: The design of materials and structures that imitate natural biological systems in form or function to improve integration with host tissues. （在形式或功能上模仿天然生物系统的材料和结构设计，旨在改善与宿主组织的整合。）
   - **关联重要名词**：*Self-assembly (自组装)* —— 分子自发形成稳定仿生结构的过程。

------

### 关联重要名词补充 (Additional Important Terms)

- **Fibronectin (纤连蛋白)**：作为“分子桥”，一端连接细胞膜蛋白（Integrin），另一端连接 ECM 蛋白。
- **Anisotropy (各向异性)**：材料性能随方向变化（如电纺丝对齐纤维），对神经、腱、肌肉等有向组织的修复至关重要。
- **Phase Separation (相分离)**：通过热力学不稳定性将聚合物溶液分为富集相和贫乏相，常结合冷冻干燥制造微孔支架。

## True&False

### 第三部分：判断题 (True & False Questions - 20题)

1. **The extracellular matrix (ECM) is composed entirely of inorganic minerals.** **细胞外基质 (ECM) 完全由无机矿物质组成。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) ECM consists of both macromolecules (proteins like collagen) and minerals. (中) ECM 由大分子（如胶原蛋白等蛋白质）和矿物质共同组成。
2. **Fibronectin acts as a bridge between the cell's cytoskeleton and the ECM.** **纤连蛋白充当细胞骨架和 ECM 之间的桥梁。**
   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) It connects to integrins in the plasma membrane, which in turn connect to microfilaments inside the cell. (中) 它连接质膜中的整联蛋白，而整联蛋白又连接细胞内部的微丝。
3. **Interconnected pores are not necessary as long as the scaffold is porous.** **只要支架有多孔性，孔隙是否连通并不重要。**
   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Pores must be interconnected to allow cell migration and the flow of nutrients/waste throughout the structure. (中) 孔隙必须连通，以允许细胞迁移以及营养/废物在整个结构中的流动。

   - **答案：False (错误)**
   - **解析：** (En) Top-down uses a pre-fabricated scaffold to dictate the shape; "Bottom-up" or self-assembly relies more on cell-driven organization. (中) 自上而下使用预制支架决定形状；“自下而上”或自组装更多依赖细胞驱动的组织。

   - **解析：** (En) It can be performed at room temperature and avoids harsh organic solvents. (中) 它可以在室温下进行，并避免了剧烈的有机溶剂。

   - **答案：True (正确)**
   - **解析：** (En) Electrospinning produces nano-scale fibers similar to natural ECM components. (中) 电纺丝产生与天然 ECM 成分相似的纳米级纤维。

## 简答

### 第四部分：简答题 (Short Answer Questions)

**1. Define the "Tissue Engineering Triad" and explain the role of each component.** **定义“组织工程三要素（三联征）”并解释每个组分的作用。**

- **答案：** The triad consists of Cells, Scaffolds, and Signals. (三要素包括细胞、支架和信号。)
  - **Cells (细胞):** The living part that synthesizes new tissue. (合成新组织的活性部分。)
  - **Scaffolds (支架):** Provide a temporary 3D template and structural support for cell growth. (为细胞生长提供临时 3D 模板和结构支撑。)
  - **Signals (信号):** Chemical (e.g., growth factors) or physical cues that guide cell behavior and differentiation. (引导细胞行为和分化的化学（如生长因子）或物理信号。)

**2. List four essential requirements for an ideal tissue engineering scaffold.** **列出理想组织工程支架的四个基本要求。**

- **答案：**
  1. Biocompatibility: Must not cause immune rejection or toxicity. (生物相容性：不得引起免疫排斥或毒性。)
  2. Biodegradability: Should degrade at a rate matching new tissue formation. (生物降解性：降解速度应与新组织形成相匹配。)
  3. Porosity: High and interconnected pores for mass transport. (孔隙率：用于质量传递的高且连通的孔隙。)
  4. Mechanical Properties: Must match the anatomical site of implantation. (机械性能：必须匹配植入的解剖部位。)

**3. Explain the "Solvent Casting and Particulate Leaching" process and its main advantage.** **解释“溶剂铸造与颗粒浸出”过程及其主要优点。**

- **答案：** A polymer is dissolved in a solvent and mixed with porogen particles (e.g., salt). After the solvent evaporates, the construct is immersed in water to leach out (dissolve) the particles, leaving a porous network. (将聚合物溶于溶剂并与致孔剂颗粒（如盐）混合。溶剂蒸发后，将构件浸入水中以浸出（溶解）颗粒，留下多孔网络。)
  - **Advantage (优点):** It is a simple and inexpensive way to control pore size and porosity by varying the particle size and amount. (通过改变颗粒大小和数量，这是一种简单且廉价的控制孔径和孔隙率的方法。)

**4. Describe the structure and function of the two types of Extracellular Matrix (ECM).** **描述两种类型的细胞外基质 (ECM) 的结构和功能。**

- **答案：**
  - **Interstitial Matrix (间质基质):** Found between intercellular spaces; forms a 3D porous gel that surrounds cells and provides support to connective tissues. (存在于细胞间隙；形成包裹细胞的 3D 多孔凝胶，为结缔组织提供支撑。)
  - **Basement Membrane (基底膜):** A sheet-like matrix found only underneath epithelial tissues; it acts as a barrier and a structural support for cell layers. (仅存在于上皮组织下方的片状基质；它充当细胞层的屏障和结构支撑。)

**5. How are hydrogels used in the engineering of a "Lab-grown Tooth"?** **水凝胶是如何在“实验室培养牙齿”的工程中使用的？**

- **答案：** Hydrogels are used as a 3D matrix to house patient-derived cells. Researchers tune the swelling and mechanical properties of the hydrogel by adjusting the ratio of synthetic to natural components. This creates a controlled environment for the cells to integrate and eventually repair themselves like a natural tooth. (水凝胶被用作容纳患者来源细胞的 3D 基质。研究人员通过调整合成与天然成分的比例来调节水凝胶的溶胀和机械性能。这为细胞创造了一个受控环境，使其能够整合并最终像天然牙齿一样自我修复。)
